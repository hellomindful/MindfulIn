A few useful answers

Before you start.

What is the difference between an audit and an assessment?

An assessment explores needs, strengths, patterns, or readiness. An audit looks more closely at how a routine, environment, system, or experience supports functioning. Your package can combine both.

Can I buy every topic as a standalone package?

The library is expanding. The $197 AIRRI — Snapshot has a live checkout. Other topics are available to explore through a package inquiry, with scope and pricing confirmed first. Standalone self-guided packages will be listed as they become available.

Can a package cover more than one topic?

Yes. Tell us which areas connect for you. A custom package can combine topics, audiences, and formats around a practical goal.

What does MESH mean?

Mental, Emotional, Social, and Health. The Mindful MESH lens helps you explore how those areas connect instead of considering each in isolation.

Is this a clinical diagnosis or treatment?

Airri packages support education, reflection, planning, and readiness. They do not provide diagnosis, psychotherapy, or clinical treatment. Trauma-informed clinical insight and neuroaffirming lenses inform the questions and planning approach.

Can I ask about accessibility or support needs?

Yes. Include any format or participation preferences in your inquiry, or contact hello@mindfulin.com before purchasing.
