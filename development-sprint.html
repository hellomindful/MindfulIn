Direct organizational inquiry

Make It Usable Development Sprint

One defined problem. One agreed resource or experience. A practical handoff your organization can use.

For agencies, learning and program organizations, clinics, schools, venues, and community teams.

Share your project brief

No purchase or commitment is required to inquire. You can request a Sprint directly without buying a review.

Agree the scope before we build

  • One audience, use case, and primary deliverable.
  • Source materials, format, accessibility needs, and acceptance criteria.
  • Included revisions, handoff, and any specialist review.
  • Project price, payment terms, delivery date, and usage rights in writing.

What happens next

We review your brief for fit and available capacity, clarify the work, and provide a bounded proposal. Work starts after scope, terms, materials, and payment arrangements are agreed.

Custom project pricing is quoted after scope review. Existing review and audit prices remain separate; they are not the Sprint price.

Ongoing support or licensing can be discussed when relevant; neither is a required next step.

What could we make more usable?

Illustrative project briefs, not client case studies or guaranteed outcomes.

A clearer program welcome

Turn scattered instructions into a participant guide with a clear first step, visual sequence, and accessible language.

A practical staff learning resource

Turn subject knowledge into one focused learning module, job aid, or facilitator resource.

A simpler visitor journey

Clarify one registration or arrival experience with a usable resource and implementation notes.

Need help deciding what to improve?

The $997 organizational review is an optional entry route. It prioritizes one touchpoint before implementation is commissioned.
