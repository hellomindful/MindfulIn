What we look for

Accessibility and participation

Making it easier for more people to access, understand, and take part.

Sensory and environmental friction

Identifying how spaces, routines, and environments affect focus, comfort, and function.

Learning and information access

Whether people can find, understand, and use what they need.

Communication and engagement

How clearly information supports participation and connection.

Usability of an experience

What makes a defined experience easier or harder to move through.

Participant and customer journeys

Barriers that appear across a specific step, process, or journey.

Questions

What can you review?

One defined organizational touchpoint, such as a website, PDF or resource, registration flow, program, event, participant journey, or communication experience.

Is this a compliance or legal audit?

No. The review is a strategic accessibility and inclusive-experience prioritization review. It is not legal advice, ADA or WCAG certification, technical code remediation, or a legal compliance determination.

How long does it take?

Your priority and action brief is delivered within five business days, followed by a 45-minute debrief.

What happens after I purchase?

You'll complete the intake so the review can be scoped to the defined touchpoint and the people it serves.

Can you help implement recommendations?

Additional implementation work can be discussed after the review when it is needed.
