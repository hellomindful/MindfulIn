About

One ecosystem, built to make complex needs easier to understand and act on.

Mindful:In is the public home of Mindful Innovations, LLC — a connected system for assessment, learning, implementation, resources, commerce, and organizational support.

Entity

Mindful Innovations, LLC

This site exists to make the company structure and pathways legible to visitors, partners, and buyers.

Founder

Michelle Swerin

MA, LPCC, ADHD-CCSP, Certified Instructional Designer, Community Health Worker.

Boundary

Clear scope

Mindful:In itself is not psychotherapy or clinical treatment delivered through this website.

Purpose

Someone has a problem. We help them understand it, learn what to do, find or make useful solutions, and stay connected. That is the through-line whether the context is one person navigating a hard morning or an organization redesigning a system.

Method

Assess, learn, apply, experience, shop, and scale — six connected capabilities that can stand alone or work together. Visitors can enter wherever fits instead of moving through a mandatory sequence.

Founder

Mindful Innovations is founded and operated by Michelle Swerin, MA, LPCC, ADHD-CCSP, Certified Instructional Designer, Community Health Worker. Her clinical and instructional-design background shapes how assessments, learning, and solutions are built here.

Experience

Everything is designed to be clear, async-friendly, and low-friction: self-paced assessments, direct routes, dedicated destinations, and straightforward next steps.

Why the structure matters

Credibility increases when people can tell what is being offered, who it is for, and where each part belongs.

What we're not

Mindful:In is not currently providing psychotherapy or other clinical treatment through this website. Assessments, audits, educational resources, roadmaps, instructional design, media, products, and consulting are informational, educational, and planning-oriented — not a diagnosis or a course of clinical treatment. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
