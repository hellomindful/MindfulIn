Make space for reflection and creativity.

Private Mindful Creative Workshops & Seminars for teams, community groups, and partner organizations.

Request a workshop proposal

Build around your audience

Guided creative workshop

Mindfulness, an adaptable art or writing prompt, and optional reflection. No previous art experience required.

Seminar + practical materials

A focused learning session with opportunities for guided practice. Discuss participant handouts, reflection prompts, or facilitator materials.

Series, partnerships & licensing

Discuss a program, repeat sessions, customized resources, or licensed materials. Scope and reuse rights are agreed for each engagement.

From inquiry to delivery

  1. Share your audience, goals, approximate group size, preferred dates, format, budget, and access needs.
  2. Receive a proposed scope covering facilitation, materials, duration, delivery arrangements, and price.
  3. Confirm the agreement, payment arrangements, venue or online access, and cancellation terms before reserving delivery.

In-person delivery depends on venue and location arrangements. Online delivery details are confirmed with the proposal.

Choose your focus

Guided mindfulness + creative reflection; mindful art and self-care; or ADHD-friendly everyday creativity. Content is adapted to the agreed audience and learning goals.

These are educational wellbeing experiences, not clinical treatment. No outcomes or credentials are awarded unless explicitly included in an agreed program.
