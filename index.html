Sensory Regulation

Our first fully connected need: sensory regulation.

Whether it's a child overwhelmed by a school morning, a workplace that's too loud to think in, or a home that never quite feels calm — sensory regulation touches nearly everything we build. It's the clearest place to see the whole ecosystem work together.
