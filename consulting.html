Mindful:In Consulting

Functional, accessible, human-centered design for organizations, experiences, learning, and environments.

We help organizations identify what is creating friction for people and turn those insights into practical action.

From assessments and learning solutions to media, resources, products, spaces, and experiences, we help make participation easier and more meaningful.

Who we work with

Mindful:In Consulting supports organizations that want to improve accessibility, participation, engagement, learning, or experience.

Education and youth organizations

Schools, education partners, youth and family organizations, and recreation programs.

Health and community organizations

Healthcare, wellness, nonprofit, and community organizations.

Public-facing organizations

Arts, libraries, cultural organizations, hospitality, and service environments.

Employers and teams

Organizations working to make communication, learning, and participation work better for people.

Growing initiatives

Teams building a more accessible, effective, or human-centered experience from the start.

Other organizations

Any organization working to reduce friction and make participation more possible.
