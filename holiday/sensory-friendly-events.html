Questions

Is this an ADA compliance review?

No. This is practical guest-experience support: quiet space, signage, communication and staff guidance. It is not legal advice or ADA certification.

Do we need a meeting?

No. Everything can be done in writing. A short call is available for Event Setup and On-Site packages if you prefer one.

What if our event is in early December?

Book now. The Quick Kit arrives in 3 business days, and Event Setup in 5.

Can we pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Choose your package, submit the form and note “invoice” or your PO details. You’ll get an invoice within 1 business day.
