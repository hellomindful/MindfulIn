What's included

The party, on every phone

A custom popover page for your team: name-based games, trivia, superlatives and a live kudos wall. Works on any phone through a QR code.

Printables + host script

Table cards, QR signs and a simple host script, so whoever runs the party knows what to say and when. Print at the office or your local printer.

Optional keepsakes

A personalized keepsake design for every employee (card, ornament or mini print), print-ready and sent to your local printer for pickup. Printing is billed by the printer.

Questions people ask

Who runs the party?

You do, with the host script. Guests play on their own phones, so there's no tech setup, no video call and nothing to download.

What if our headcount changes?

Book with your best estimate. You can update the number up to 3 days before the party; added guests are invoiced at $12 each.

Is it accessible?

Yes. Large readable text, high contrast, optional participation and no timed pressure. Tell us about any specific needs in your brief and we'll design around them.

Can it work for a remote or hybrid team?

Yes. Everything runs on phones through a link or QR code, so people can join from anywhere.

How do we pay?

By Stripe invoice: card or bank transfer. Applicable sales tax is added to the invoice.

What about after the holidays?

The same format works for New Year kickoffs, Galentine's, birthdays and team milestones. Ask about a seasonal plan for your team.
