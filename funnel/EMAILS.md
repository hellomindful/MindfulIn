# Funnel Follow-Up Emails

Load these into HubSpot (Automation → Sequences or Workflows), or send manually from templates. Keep each email short and plain, with one link. Replace `[brackets]`.

---

## A. Order received (send within 1 business day, or right away if automated)

**Trigger:** any `order-*` form submission

**Subject:** Your [offer name] is reserved

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thank you. Your [option name] for [organization or recipient] is reserved.
>
> **Next step:** complete your [payment / 50% deposit] here: [payment link]
> (Prefer an invoice or PO? Just reply with the details.)
>
> Once that's in, here's what happens: [delivery promise from the offer page].
>
> Michelle
> Mindful:In · hello@mindfulin.com

## B. Payment reminder (24 hours after A, if unpaid)

**Subject:** Holding your spot until [date]

> Hi [first name], just a quick note that your [option name] is still reserved. I'm holding the spot until [date + 2 days], since [orders close November 20 / packing slots are limited].
>
> Pay here: [payment link], or reply if you need an invoice.

## C. Last call (72 hours after A, if unpaid)

**Subject:** Should I release your spot?

> Hi [first name], I haven't seen payment come through for your [option name]. Should I keep it for you? Reply "keep it" and I'll send a fresh link, or "release" and I'll free it up. Either is completely fine.

## D. Paid: welcome and intake (right after payment)

**Subject:** You're booked. Here's what I need from you.

> Thank you, [first name]. You're booked.
>
> To get started, reply with: [event plan or venue photos / delivery contact / anything the intake form didn't cover].
>
> You'll have [deliverable] by [date].

## E. Delivered + cross-sell (on delivery)

**Subject:** Your [deliverable] is here

> Here's your [deliverable]: [link or tracking]
>
> If anything needs adjusting, reply and I'll fix it.
>
> One more thing for the season: [cross-sell one line, e.g. "Many teams pair their event with Desk Reset stockings for staff, from $380 for 10."] [link]

## F. Review request (5 days after delivery)

**Subject:** One quick question

> How did it go? A sentence or two about what worked would mean a lot. With your OK, I may share it (first name and organization type only).
