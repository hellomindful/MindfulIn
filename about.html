About

One ecosystem, built around real needs.

Mindful Innovations exists to take real-world needs and turn them into practical assessments, learning, products, experiences, and scalable solutions. Not a mandatory funnel — an ecosystem you can enter from wherever fits.

Purpose

Someone has a problem. We help them understand it, learn what to do, find or make useful solutions, and stay connected. That's the whole method, whether the "someone" is an individual navigating a hard morning or an organization redesigning a space.

Method

Assess, learn, apply, experience, shop, and scale — six capabilities that each stand on their own, and that connect to one another as needed. We don't require every visitor to move through every step.

Founder

Mindful Innovations is founded and operated by Michelle Swerin, MA, LPCC, ADHD-CCSP, Certified Instructional Designer, Community Health Worker. Her clinical and instructional-design background informs how the assessments, learning, and solutions here are built — but Mindful:In itself is not a therapy practice, and nothing on this site is psychotherapy, diagnosis, or clinical treatment.

Experience

Everything here is designed to be async, clear, and low-friction — self-paced assessments, digital delivery, and straightforward next steps, without requiring a live call just to get started.

What we're not

Mindful:In is not currently providing psychotherapy or other clinical treatment through this website. Assessments, audits, educational resources, roadmaps, instructional design, media, products, and consulting are informational, educational, and planning-oriented — not a diagnosis or a course of clinical treatment. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
