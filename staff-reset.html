Train a team · Educational, not therapy

A practical reset your team can use.

Staff Reset Workshop — $450 for one online session for up to 25 people. Choose one focus, such as busy-day resets, competition-day routines, or a short creative practice.

Request a workshop date

What is included

  • One 60-minute guided workshop.
  • A team handout and up to three reusable prompt or cue sheets from existing templates.
  • Four weekly follow-up emails to one team contact.
  • Up to two questions from that contact about the delivered materials during 30 days.

In-person workshops are welcome. Travel, venue costs, art supplies, extra people, recordings, and extra design work are quoted before booking. Team handouts are for your team's use; resale and facilitator licensing are separate.

Holiday version

Use the workshop to help your team plan for a busy holiday period. A proposed $1,300 package combines the workshop with 25 desk-reset stockings. Stocking contents, actual costs, location, packing capacity, and delivery must be confirmed before we accept payment. No stocking inventory or shipping date is promised by this page.

How to book

  1. Send your audience, group size, preferred dates, format, goal, and access needs.
  2. We confirm the session, materials, travel if needed, price, and cancellation terms.
  3. Sign the agreement and pay the required invoice to confirm your date.

An inquiry does not reserve a date. A larger audit or Readiness Review is a different service; it is not included in this workshop.

What 30 days of support means

Four short weekly emails help you use what we made. Your named team contact may send up to two questions about the delivered plan or materials during those 30 days. We reply within two business days. New projects, extra sessions, urgent help, and ongoing personal messaging are not included.

Support starts on the workshop date. Your proposal confirms any different timing. This is educational support, not therapy or crisis care.
