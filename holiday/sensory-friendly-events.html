For organizations

Written support for a calmer event

Event Setup is $697 for one agreed event. I make the plan and materials. Your team handles printing and on-site setup.

What you receive

  • A written review of your event plan.
  • A quiet-space layout for the venue information you provide.
  • A guest “what to expect” page and printable sign files.
  • A staff and volunteer tip sheet.
  • One round of written revisions.

Start with a request

Send your event date, venue, audience, and what you need. We will confirm fit, available capacity, scope, payment arrangements, and a delivery date before accepting the work. This inquiry is not a paid booking.

Printing, equipment, travel, and on-site setup are not included. The Quick Kit and on-site package are not currently open for new orders.
