For adults · Educational support

Make one stuck part of your day easier.

Reset Session + 30 Days — $397. We choose one everyday problem, make a small plan together, and give you tools to try it.

Ask about a Reset Session

No payment is needed to ask. We confirm fit, scope, a date, and payment before booking.

What you get

  • One 60-minute online session focused on one everyday task or routine.
  • A short personal plan and up to three simple tools drawn from existing templates.
  • Four weekly follow-up emails and up to two questions about your plan.

Your kit may include a checklist, visual cue, prompt, or link to a useful tool. It does not include a new app, clinical assessment, medication advice, or a full home or school plan.

How it works

  1. Email the everyday task you want help with and your preferred times. Please do not include medical records or private health details.
  2. We confirm whether this educational service fits. You receive the agreed scope, booking terms, and invoice.
  3. After payment and the short brief are complete, we meet and make your plan. Your written kit follows within two business days.

Need clinical assessment or treatment? Ask for the separate secure clinical route. This session is educational, not therapy.

What 30 days of support means

Four short weekly emails help you use what we made. You may send up to two questions about the delivered plan or materials during those 30 days. We reply within two business days. New projects, extra sessions, urgent help, and ongoing personal messaging are not included.

Support starts on your session date. Your proposal confirms any different timing. This is educational support, not therapy or crisis care.
