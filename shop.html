Explore Solutions

Connected from concept to delivery.

Space is the design, production and product-fulfillment arm serving both Mindful Empowered and Mindful:In Human Design. Studios contributes creative assets, stories and media. Solutions contributes learning design, guidance and implementation.

For a commissioned community art experience, Space can coordinate materials and production, Studios the artwork and media, and Solutions the participant resources. One lead arm owns the agreed delivery scope.

Manufacturing method, supplier availability, samples, shipping, installation requirements, price and timing are confirmed for each offer before commitment. A custom concept is not automatically an available shop product.
