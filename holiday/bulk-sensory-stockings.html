Inside every stocking

Six item types, packed the same way every time

Brands vary with supply, so we describe items by type rather than promising a specific brand.

  • 1 slow-rise squishy
  • 1 textured or click fidget
  • 1 aromatherapy item
  • 1 tea or cocoa packet
  • 1 comfort item (eye mask or soft socks)
  • 1 printed 60-second reset card
