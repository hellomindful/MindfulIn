Questions

What if I don’t know their sensory preferences?

Share what you do know. I’ll lean toward gentle, adjustable picks and note anything worth checking.

Are the links affiliate links?

Some may be. Picks are chosen for the person first, never for the commission.

Can I do more than one person?

Yes. Add up to 4 recipients at $29 each and describe each person in the notes.
